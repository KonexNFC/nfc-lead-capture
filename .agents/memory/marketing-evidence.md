Do not add estimated usage counters, invented ROI claims, or sample customer endorsements to the public website.

**Why:** The previous homepage included hardcoded marketing statistics and fictional fallback testimonials. An approved feed can also contain demo records; approval alone does not establish authenticity.

**How to apply:** Use substantiated claims and genuine approved customer quotes. Keep demo records out of marketing without deleting database records.
