What is an NFC Business Card?
An NFC business card contains a small embedded chip that can wirelessly transmit data to a smartphone when tapped. Unlike traditional paper cards, NFC cards allow for dynamic content — including contact info, websites, social media profiles, portfolios, reviews and more — all accessible with just one tap.  
Materials of NFC Business Cards
We produce NFC cards in a range of materials to meet different branding and durability needs:
* PVC (Plastic) The most common material, offering durability, water resistance, and a smooth professional finish. Ideal for daily use and large-scale distribution.
* Metal Premium and heavy-duty, metal NFC cards provide a luxurious feel and high-end brand image. Available in stainless steel, brushed metal, or matte black finishes.
* Wood Eco-friendly and elegant, wooden NFC cards are perfect for brands that value sustainability and a natural aesthetic. Common wood types include bamboo, walnut, and maple.
