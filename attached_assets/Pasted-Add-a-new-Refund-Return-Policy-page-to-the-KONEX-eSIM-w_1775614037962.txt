Refund & Return Policy

Overview
KONEX eSIM provides digital, prepaid eSIM data plans delivered electronically upon successful purchase. Because our products are digital services that are fulfilled instantly, refund eligibility is limited.

Non-Refundable Situations
Refunds will not be provided in the following cases:
- The eSIM has been activated, installed, or scanned
- Any data usage has occurred, whether partial or full
- The customer selected the wrong destination, duration, or plan
- The customer’s device is not eSIM-compatible
- The purchase was made by mistake or the customer changed their mind after delivery

Eligible Refund Situations
A refund may be considered only if:
- The eSIM has not been activated, installed, or used
- The QR code or activation details were not successfully delivered
- There is a verified technical issue that prevents activation or normal use
- The service delivered was materially different from what was purchased

Technical Support Before Refund
If a customer experiences an activation or connectivity issue, KONEX eSIM support will first investigate and attempt to resolve the issue. Customers should contact support as soon as possible with their order details and device information. If the issue cannot be resolved and the service was not successfully delivered, a partial or full refund may be issued at our discretion.

Refund Request Window
All refund requests must be submitted within 7 days of purchase. Requests made after this period may not be eligible for review.

How Refunds Are Processed
Approved refunds will be issued to the original payment method only. Refund processing times may vary depending on the payment provider and bank, but typically take 5 to 10 business days.

Fraud, Abuse, and Misuse
KONEX eSIM reserves the right to refuse refunds where there is evidence of misuse, repeated abuse of the refund process, or fraudulent activity.

Contact

support@konexesim.com
