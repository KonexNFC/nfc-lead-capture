PRD: KONEX NFC – Product Overview Section (Website)

1. Objective

Add a high-conversion Product Overview section to the KONEX website that clearly explains the value of KONEX NFC cards and presents the three product tiers (Basic, Premium, Elite) with clear CTAs.

This section should educate first-time visitors, position KONEX as a premium NFC solution, and route users into tier-specific product pages or a waitlist/preorder flow.

⸻

2. Scope

Build a new, responsive Product Overview module to be added to the homepage and accessible at /konex-cards.

Includes:
	•	Hero introduction to KONEX NFC
	•	Three-tier product cards (Basic, Premium, Elite)
	•	Key benefits strip
	•	Comparison snapshot
	•	Primary CTAs (Order / Join Waitlist)

Out of scope (Phase 2):
	•	Payments & checkout
	•	User profile dashboard
	•	NFC profile management

⸻

3. User Stories
	•	As a visitor, I want to quickly understand what KONEX NFC is.
	•	As a professional, I want to see which card tier fits my needs.
	•	As a buyer, I want a clear call-to-action to order or join a waitlist.
	•	As a mobile user, I want the section to be fully responsive and fast-loading.

⸻

4. Page Structure & Components

4.1 Hero Section

Component: KonexHero

Content
	•	Headline: Tap once. Share everything.
	•	Subheadline: KONEX NFC cards replace paper business cards with instant digital profiles.
	•	Primary CTA: Get your KONEX card
	•	Secondary CTA: Join the waitlist

Acceptance Criteria
	•	CTA buttons route to /konex-cards and /waitlist (or modal).
	•	Optimized for mobile (headline max 2 lines on mobile).

⸻

4.2 Product Tier Cards

Component: KonexTierGrid

Each tier displayed as a card with:
	•	Tier name
	•	Positioning line
	•	3–5 key benefits
	•	CTA button

Tiers

Basic
	•	Title: KONEX Basic
	•	Tagline: Simple. Smart. Always connected.
	•	CTA: Order KONEX Basic → /konex-cards/basic

Premium
	•	Title: KONEX Premium
	•	Tagline: Elevated presence. Premium impression.
	•	CTA: Upgrade to Premium → /konex-cards/premium

Elite
	•	Title: KONEX Elite
	•	Tagline: For those who move differently.
	•	CTA: Access Elite → /konex-cards/elite

Acceptance Criteria
	•	Cards must be equal height.
	•	Hover state shows subtle elevation.
	•	Mobile stacks vertically.

⸻

4.3 Key Benefits Strip

Component: KonexBenefits

Bullets
	•	One-tap instant contact sharing
	•	Works on iPhone & Android
	•	No app required for recipients
	•	Update your profile anytime
	•	Eco-friendly alternative to paper cards

Acceptance Criteria
	•	Render as icons + text.
	•	Responsive grid (2x2 mobile, 5-column desktop).

⸻

4.4 Comparison Snapshot

Component: KonexComparisonMini

Layout
	•	3-column comparison: Basic / Premium / Elite
	•	3 bullet points per tier

Acceptance Criteria
	•	Not a full pricing table, just a positioning snapshot.
	•	Clickable to tier pages.

⸻

4.5 Final CTA Section

Component: KonexFinalCTA

Copy
	•	Headline: The last business card you’ll ever need.
	•	CTA: Get your KONEX card

Acceptance Criteria
	•	High-contrast CTA.
	•	Sticky CTA on mobile optional.

⸻

5. Routes
	•	/konex-cards → Product overview page
	•	/konex-cards/basic
	•	/konex-cards/premium
	•	/konex-cards/elite
